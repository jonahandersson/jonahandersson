## About me

I am a **Principal Cloud Architect**, **Microsoft MVP**, **Microsoft Certified Trainer Community Lead**, and **.NET & Azure expert** passionate about building modern cloud solutions, helping developers grow, and inspiring others through technology.

I am the author of O'Reilly's **[Learning Microsoft Azure](https://www.learningmicrosoftazure.com)**, founder of **[Azure User Group Sweden](https://www.meetup.com/azureusergroupsundsvallsverige/)**, and a **2x Developer of the Year winner**. I also advocate for **women in tech in Sweden and across the Nordics**.

As an **international speaker**, I have presented at conferences across **Europe, the United States, and globally**, including **Microsoft Ignite Chicago 2024**. I am currently speaking at **Microsoft Build 2026 in San Francisco**, where I will present **[From Code to Agents: Build Production MCP Servers on Azure Functions](https://build.microsoft.com/en-US/sessions/LTG455?source=/speakers/1768245294609build26-1776293017373001FIeV)**.

I am also a **LinkedIn Instructor**, host of the **[ExtendWomenInTech Podcast](https://open.spotify.com/show/3bXdNjZYomEg2FEwUkEFXB)**, and share content with **16,000+ followers on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jonahandersson)**.
